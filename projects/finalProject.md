This is the Final Project from this course, ICS 314, that I worked on within a group of 3, me included. It was supposed to be a website where users could explore and interact with the clubs that UH Manoa offers, as opposed to the current set-up, which is a Google Spreadsheet.

It was a web application that allowed users to sign in as either a Student, a Club Officer, or a Site Administrator. And depending on the role, users would be given a number of elements, such as the ability to manage your personal dashboard / profile, manage your club's details like description and meeting times / locations, or managing the clubs on the site in general (at least in terms of approval or rejection).

While it was an okay project to work on, I do with I could've gotten either more time or more direction. There was quite a bit that I wanted to implement into my code, such as a proper Sign-In page and more functional features. Maybe even a few images that weren't placeholders. One of the few things I personally was interested in, but I was both unsure how to implement and unable to do so within the given time, was implementing a way for users to change the themes and style of the website to however they would like. Visually speaking, of course, by being given a number of different colors and text fonts, maybe even a dark mode.

But alas, there's more to a learning experience that having grievances; I'd believe that we were unprepared to properly deal with the workload, and there's nothing really wrong with that. That just means we'll have to work a bit harder and a bit better next time. Look on the bright side and all that jazz, the best part of having pot-holes is that now you know where you need to fill in the ditches.
