## What a week, huh? (Captain, it's Monday)

To be honest, I hadn't known we were supposed to be making an effort estimation log for the Final Project, similar to what was done for the course assignments, until my group began to address Milestone 3. As a result, most of the durations for estimations and coding effort were mostly approximations as how long certain tasks took to work on them.

However, in a scenario that we were more aware of making our effort estimation log, I believe that making duration estimations in advance would provide a sense of perspective, ranging from Milestone 1 to Milestone 3. I personally found the effort estimation log to very beneficial in making me faster and more proficient in coding when I recorded in it for the course assignments, so I believe it would've benefited us as a group to have such a log — it would definitely help us with managing time better, seeing which task took more or less time and working our personal schedules arounds similarly difficult tasks. And while our actual estimations are mostly approximations, we probably would've used timers to measure how long a task took; however, for tasks that didn't really require coding necessarily, we likely would've made estimations of tasks that more or less took multiple days.

This project was fairly difficult for me personally, partially because I found myself lost in how to implement certain concepts and functions, and partially because I believe that my group had a bit of difficult working together in terms of conflicting schedules and lack of communication at times. And while it didn't stop us from having something to deploy, I did feel like we could've added so much more than what we had. But I suppose that just makes this Final Project into a big learning experience for next time.

## In Regards to AI Usage

For the Final Project, I did find myself utilizing an AI assistant for problematic scenarios in which I couldn't find solutions to easily online. For example, I was tasked with implementing the Playwright tests for our website application, but I didn't really have any prior knowledge or experience in doing so. I was able to use a bit of the code from the [nextjs applicaion template](https://github.com/ics-software-engineering/nextjs-application-template) and from scouring some online sources, but I need to troubleshoot a few errors with Google Gemini in order to get things running smoothly. And while I've never heard of it before, if I were to do this project again, I probably would've utilized WakaTime for recording how long working on code took, if but to make my life just a bit easier.

For the record, I did not use AI to make this essay.
